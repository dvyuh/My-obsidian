#### Design a digital ecosystem for 'SUB-LEVEL', an app that functions like a digital zine for local, small-scale social gatherings. The app should prioritize motion and atmosphere, using a vertical 'mood-board' feed instead of traditional lists. The responsive website must act as a 'Post-Event Archive,' where posters and photos from past themed parties are preserved in an experimental, collage-style layout. Style guide: Sharp edges, overlapping elements, and tactile, analog-inspired interactions
